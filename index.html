📖 Rules of Seep (सीप) & Strategy Guide

Seep (also spelled Sweep, Sip, or Shiv) is a beloved traditional partnership card game popular across Northern India (Punjab, Haryana, Delhi, Uttar Pradesh, Rajasthan) and Pakistan.


The Setup & Objective:

  • 4 Players: Played in fixed partnerships of 2 (North + South vs East + West).
  • Standard 52-Card Deck: Cards from Ace to King.
  • Objective: Capture point cards from the table layout (the "floor" or "farsh").
  • Baazi (Match Victory): The match ends when a team accumulates a lead of at least 100 points over the opposing team.

Card Capture Values:

For capturing and building, card numerical values are:

Ace = 1, 2 through 10 = face value, Jack = 11, Queen = 12, King = 13.

Out of 52 cards, only 17 cards carry points, summing to exactly 100 points per round:

Suit / Card Points Value Notes
♠ All 13 Spades (Hukum) Equal to rank (1 to 13) ♠A=1, ♠2=2 ... ♠10=10, ♠J=11, ♠Q=12, ♠K=13 (Total = 91 pts)
♦ Ten of Diamonds (Eent ki Dehla) 6 Points The most prized individual card outside Spades!
♥ Ace of Hearts (Paan ka Ikka) 1 Point Point card
♣ Ace of Clubs (Chidi ka Ikka) 1 Point Point card
♦ Ace of Diamonds (Eent ka Ikka) 1 Point Point card
Total Deck Points 100 Points All other 35 cards are worth 0 points!

Piles of cards grouped together on the table are called Houses (Ghar). Houses can only be created with values from 9 to 13.


1. Building a House (Kacha Ghar):

To build a house of value H (9 to 13), you play a card from your hand onto loose floor card(s) such that their sum equals H. Requirement: You must hold at least one OTHER card of value H in your hand to prove you can capture it later!


2. Cementing a House (Pakka Ghar):

An ordinary house is vulnerable. Adding another card or combination of the same value H cements it (Pakka). Once cemented, it is locked: opponents cannot alter or raise it; it can only be captured with an H card!


3. Breaking / Raising a House:

An uncemented (Kacha) house owned by the opposing team can be raised to a higher value (up to 13) by adding a card from hand, provided you hold the card of the new higher value!

⚡ What is a SEEP (Sweep)?

A Seep occurs when a player's capture completely clears the table layout, leaving ZERO loose cards and ZERO houses on the floor!


• Normal Seep Bonus: +50 Points!

• Opening Move Seep: If scored on the very first play of the deal: +25 Points.

• Last Turn of Deal: Capturing cards on the final play does NOT count as a Seep bonus (the capturing team simply collects all remaining cards).


Scoring multiple Seeps in a match is the fastest and most thrilling way to win a Baazi!

Traditional North Indian Seep Terminology:

  • Farsh (फ़र्श): The floor / table where loose cards and houses lie.
  • Boli (बोली / Chowka): The opening 4-card deal and mandatory bid (9-13).
  • Kacha Ghar (कच्चा घर): An uncemented house that can be raised by opponents.
  • Pakka Ghar (पक्का घर): A cemented house locked against raises.
  • Seep / Shiv (सीप): A sweep that clears all cards from the table.
  • Baazi (बाज़ी): Winning the match by reaching a 100-point lead.
  • Hukum (हुकुम): Spades (♠).
  • Eent (ईंट): Diamonds (♦).
  • Paan (पान): Hearts (♥).
  • Chidi (चिड़ी): Clubs (♣).
  • Dehla (दहला): Ten of Diamonds (worth 6 points).
